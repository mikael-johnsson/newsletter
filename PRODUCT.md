# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Readers:** people who follow one author and want that author's writing in their inbox. They meet the product on the public sign-up page and afterwards mostly in their email client.
- **The author:** one person who writes the newsletter, sends issues and manages their own subscriber list.

## Product Purpose

A personal newsletter: one author writing to their own readership. The product lets readers subscribe, lets the author write and send issues by email, and lets the author manage the subscriber list. Success means readers sign up and the author can publish without friction.

## Positioning

Open decision: what makes this newsletter worth subscribing to, compared with any other, has not been stated. Do not invent a tagline or value claim; ask the author.

## Operating Context

- Readers sign up on a public web page, then read issues as email.
- The author works in a private editor/admin area to write issues, send them and see subscribers.
- Sending goes through a mail provider (not yet chosen).

## Capabilities and Constraints

In scope:

- Public sign-up page
- Editor / admin for writing issues and managing subscribers
- Sending issues by email

Out of scope for now: a public web archive of past issues.

Undecided: the mail provider, the deploy target, the newsletter's name and its topic.

Never send real email or touch the production subscriber list during development.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet: no subscriber counts, testimonials, past issues or press. Future work must not fabricate any of them.

## Product Principles

1. **One voice.** It is one person's newsletter; the product serves that author and their readers, not a platform of many writers.
2. **Subscribing is the whole job of the public page.** Nothing on it should compete with signing up.
3. **The email is the product readers keep.** Issues must read well in an inbox, not only on the web.
4. **Respect the list.** Subscriber data and sending are handled carefully; no accidental sends.
